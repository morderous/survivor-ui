The launcher's art

Frames, buttons, switches, the title plaque, the progress and scroll bars and
the window's buttons are from "Assets: UI RPG Game Interface [Lite Version]"
by Wenrexa, https://wenrexa.itch.io/werdanta03, under Creative Commons Zero
v1.0 Universal (CC0, public domain dedication): free to use, change and
share, no credit required. Bought by morderous on 2026-10-25; credited here
with thanks.

make_art.py copies the pieces used from the pack's PNG folder under plain
names, and makes two from them: inset.png (Menu02 without its title band)
and arrow-down.png (BtnIcon07 turned down).

The title's font, Cinzel Decorative, is under the SIL Open Font License
(OFL-CinzelDecorative.txt, beside the launcher's source). The stone of the
header, the coffee cup and the rest are drawn by the launcher itself.
