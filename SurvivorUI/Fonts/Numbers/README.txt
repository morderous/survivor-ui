Fonts for the game's own damage numbers (Combat text, The game's numbers).

Scaled copies, at 90% down to 40% by tens, of fonts in the folder above, subset to Latin
letters, their hinting and kerning left out, renamed "SUI Numbers <name> <size>":

  Barlow-*.ttf  Barlow Condensed SemiBold  (OFL-Barlow.txt)
  Exo-*.ttf     Exo SemiBold               (OFL-Exo.txt)
  Fira-*.ttf    Fira Sans Medium           (OFL-FiraSans.txt)
  Noto-*.ttf    Noto Sans Medium           (OFL-NotoSans.txt)

Each stays under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, as its original.
